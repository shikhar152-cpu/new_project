# XLEY V2 — Higgsfield image prompts (concept art, pending approval)

These are the written design prompts used to generate concept images for
`output/XLEY_Brand_Strategy_V2.pdf`. They were written before any image was
generated, as the brief requires. Every output is **exploratory concept art**,
not an approved character, name or asset. Final characters need founder,
product and legal sign-off, plus a proper character sheet from the design team.

- Tool: Higgsfield MCP connector (connected to the user's account in this session)
- Model: `nano_banana_pro` (Google), 2k resolution, text-to-image; later shots
  use earlier outputs as image references to keep the characters consistent
- Approval: the user asked for Higgsfield generation in their request
- Rules applied to every prompt: no text, no logos, no money imagery,
  nothing scary, no copying of existing mascots (Meta, Grok, Dots or others)
  or of the people in the AI-influencer reference poster

## Shared style tokens

> Premium stylized 3D animated feature-film render, soft materials, rounded
> silhouettes, gentle studio lighting with a warm key light and a mint rim
> light. Palette: Midnight Plum #241B36, Mint Teal #56C6B7, Soft Sand #EADCCB,
> Electric Lime #B7E64C (sparse accent), Cloud White #F7F5F1. No text, no
> logos, no watermark.

## 1. XEXE — whole-app companion (hero, 4:5)

Full-body design of XEXE, an original mascot: a small, chubby, huggable
gorilla-inspired companion with the softness of a plush teddy bear. Rounded
pear-shaped silhouette, a big soft head about 40% of body height, short stubby
legs, soft arms with rounded mitten hands. Warm cocoa-brown plush fur with a
lighter oat-coloured face, chest patch and palms; a small sandy tuft on top of
the head (a nod to the current mascot). Wide gentle muzzle, large friendly
dark-brown eyes with big catchlights, a soft brow ridge as the only gorilla
cue, tiny rounded nose, small closed-mouth smile, faintly flushed cheeks. Thin
round mint-teal glasses. Soft oversized midnight-plum puffer-bomber with
mint-teal ribbed cuffs and collar, a small round electric-lime badge with a
simple X shape, cream tee, cross-body mint satchel with a rounded X clasp (the
"brings campaigns to you" prop from v1.2, evolved). Three-quarter stance, one
hand on the satchel strap, the other giving a small reassuring wave, head
slightly tilted. Not scary, no teeth, not a realistic ape, not a monkey in
streetwear. Seamless soft-sand background with a soft floor shadow.

## 2. XEXE — expression strip (16:9, uses #1 as reference)

Four head-and-shoulders poses of the same character in a row, same scale and
lighting on soft sand: (1) warm wave hello, (2) thinking, finger on chin,
(3) celebrating, both arms up, eyes happily closed, (4) reassuring, hand on
heart, soft smile.

## 3. XOXO — AI-experience character, three explorations (1:1)

Shared: an original AI companion for a creator app, made to sit beside a plush
gorilla-teddy mascot without competing with it. Small, clever, calm, quietly
magical. Centred on a seamless midnight-plum background with a soft vignette.
No robot clichés, no humanoid body.

- **3A "Glow Drop"**: a palm-sized droplet/bean-shaped being of frosted
  mint-teal jelly-glass with soft inner light, two oval eyes made of light, a
  tiny curved smile, stubby nub arms, a small electric-lime spark floating
  above its head like a thought, hovering over a soft glow pool.
- **3B "Orbit"**: a small floating sphere with a soft matte cloud-white body,
  two capsule-shaped light eyes, a thin glowing mint-teal ring orbiting it
  diagonally, and a faint lime X-shaped light core inside the body.
- **3C "Fold"**: a tiny floating stack of soft, rounded, card-like panels in
  mint teal and cloud white that fan open like wings, with a simple face on
  the top card and faint lime light along the edges (cards = scripts,
  briefs, campaigns).

## 4. Duet key visual (16:9, uses #1 and the chosen XOXO as references)

Cinematic 3D animated film still. A young Indian woman creator sits
cross-legged on her bed in a cosy small bedroom studio at dusk (ring light,
phone on a mini tripod, fairy lights, plants, warm lamp), smiling with relief
at her phone. XEXE sits close with an arm around her shoulder. XOXO floats in
front of the phone, sending a soft card of light toward the screen. The
creator is the protagonist: largest and sharpest in frame.

## 5. Mini-series storyboard frames (16:9, use #4 as reference)

- **Ep 1 "Meet XEXE"**: the same creator at night, overwhelmed; her phone is
  flooded with blurred chat bubbles and notification badges; she rubs her
  temples. XEXE arrives gently and offers a warm side-hug; soft light enters.
- **Ep 2 "XEXE meets XOXO"**: XEXE, curious, leans toward the phone as tiny
  luminous XOXO peeks out from behind the screen for the first time; the
  creator watches in wonder in soft focus.
- **Ep 3 "The First Mission"**: the creator at a desk drafting a script.
  XOXO floats beside the screen as glowing blank cards arrange into a neat
  outline (no readable text). XEXE gives a proud thumbs-up.
- **Ep 4 "Seeds of the crew"**: XEXE, XOXO and the creator on a city
  rooftop at dusk; a third small, undefined glowing silhouette among the
  stars. A hint, not a character.

Shared: an original virtual influencer, hyper-stylized and clearly CG (smooth
stylized skin, slightly exaggerated proportions, not a real person),
editorial chest-up portrait, direct gaze, odd and instantly memorable
silhouette. Energy borrowed from the reference poster; no faces, hairstyles,
outfits or typography copied from it.

- **6A "Tripod Tinku"** (working name): a lanky young Indian man with a

  round mint-teal glasses, midnight-plum collarless kurta-shirt over a cream
  tee, holding a phone on a mini tripod like a microphone. Electric-lime
  background.
- **6B "Noor Nazar"** (working name): a young Indian woman with a glossy,
  sharp, asymmetric chin-length midnight-plum bob and one electric-lime
  streak, one eyebrow raised in playful doubt, a small silver nose stud, an
  oversized mint-teal tailored blazer, a mint acrylic gavel resting against
  her cheek ("Trend Court" host). Soft-sand background.
