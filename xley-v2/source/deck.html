Materials & motion

XEXE = plush: soft fur, warm key light, weighty and slow.

Motion eases in and out with a "breathing" idle. No shakes, no flashing. Keep at least 30% negative space.

Accessibility

Colour is never the only signal. Every character state has a text label. Reduced motion shows a still pose. Captions on every film, plus regional-language subtitles and Hindi/English variants of key screens.

Positioning adapts v1.2 §2 ("India's Instagram and YouTube creators, from nano to next-gen, in every niche and every city"). Contrast ratios computed with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula. Fonts: the deck is set in Baloo 2 and Nunito Sans; final brand fonts need approval.
